slay is the composition-guide camera for iPhone. Alongside the app, we publish five original cinematic looks as free `.cube` LUTs. You can use them in personal and commercial work under [CC BY 4.0](https://github.com/slaycamera/luts/blob/main/LICENSE).

Each LUT is a 33×33×33 3D LUT. It loads into CapCut, DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, Photoshop, After Effects and Affinity Photo. You can also apply it yourself with a few lines of code.

## The looks

Each preview shows the original photo on the left and the graded photo on the right.

| Look | File | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **slay Amber**: golden-hour glow | `slay-01-amber.cube` | Golden hour, travel, outdoor portraits |
| **slay Fade**: matte film fade | `slay-02-fade.cube` | Moody stills, subtle grading |
| **slay Neon**: night-city punch | `slay-03-neon.cube` | City lights, neon, night photos |
| **slay Film**: classic filmic | `slay-04-film.cube` | Everyday photos, portraits, video |
| **slay Portrait**: soft and flattering | `slay-05-portrait.cube` | People, selfies, close-ups |
